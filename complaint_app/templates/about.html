About Project

"Helping Students Report Issues Safely and Easily Through Technology."

Our Objective

Many students and young people aged 13 to 25 experience issues like harassment, cyberbullying, academic injustice, or physical safety concerns, yet hesitate to report them. The fear of reprisal, complicated reporting procedures, and a lack of clear communication channels prevent them from seeking help.

ReportSafe was built to remove these barriers. By combining simple reporting flows, absolute data privacy protection, and transparent tracking mechanisms, we empower students to flag problems securely and confidently.

How It Works

1. Submit

Fill out a simple complaint form. Upload evidence documents or images. Choose to submit anonymously or under your student profile.

2. Track

Receive a unique Complaint Tracking ID. Check status updates in real-time as administrators review the submission.

3. Resolve

Administrators update the status and write responses or instructions to resolve the matter safely and effectively.

Support Services & Hotlines

If you are in immediate danger or need counseling support, please reach out to professional services:

  • Childline India: Dial 1098 (24x7 free tele-counseling and emergency support for kids/youth).
  • Kiran Mental Health Helpline: Dial 1800-599-0019 (Government support hotline for mental wellness).
  • Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: Visit cybercrime.gov.in to report severe online threats directly.
