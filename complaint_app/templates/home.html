What Issues Can You Report?

Choose from our main reporting categories to submit your concern directly to administrators.

Harassment

Report any form of harassment (online or offline), physical intimidation, or threat from anyone in or out of your institution.

Bullying / Cyberbullying

Speak up against school yard intimidation or toxic digital behavior, online abuse, social media defamation, or hacking.

Safety Issues

Flag physical threats on campus, unsafe commutes, stalking, or immediate hazard issues requiring immediate attention.

College/School Related

Address academic misconduct, unfair treatment by faculty or staff, administrative hurdles, or classroom facilities.

Civic Issues

Report public problems impacting your commute to school or college (unpaved roads, broken streetlights, trash pile-ups).

Why ReportSafe?

Fully Anonymous Option

You don't need to log in or share any identifying details. We generate a tracking code so you can check progress without revealing your identity.

Data Protection & Privacy

All registered student database fields are encrypted. Administrators process issues standardly without exposing student emails or numbers.

Transparent Tracking

Follow your complaint progress as it moves from Submitted, to Under Review, to In Progress, and finally Resolved.
